# Limitations

1. **No possession-timestamped Goals Added** after entry — ASA provides game-level G+ for minutes played.  
2. **xG model disagreement** — FotMob shotmap xG and ASA xG are different estimators.  
3. **Attribution confounding** — multi-player substitutions, score effects, and opponent behavior are partially controlled, not eliminated.  
4. **Identity bridging** — ASA↔FotMob name matching can miss edge cases.  
5. **Sample size** — substitute minutes are sparse; shrinkage and eligibility thresholds are required.  
6. **Not causal** — results are associations from public data.
